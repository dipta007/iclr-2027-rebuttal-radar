    What do reviewers like most?

    Compare problems with:

    Each box shows how many reviews said accept (a rating of 3 or 4). Reviewers give three extra scores: soundness, informativeness, and presentation. Each goes from 1 (lowest) to 3. The words here are short names for the three levels, not the exact text of the review form. One reviewer gives all of them at once, so this shows what goes together, not what causes what.
